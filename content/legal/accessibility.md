Songcry is built so people can find artists rising in their own city. That should be true for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, who need larger text, who navigate without precise touch, or who rely on captions.

We are not finished. This page says plainly where we are today, what we know does not work yet, and how to reach a person if something blocks you.

## 1. What this covers

This statement covers the Songcry mobile app and this website at songcry.app.

## 2. The standard we are working to

We are working toward **WCAG 2.2 Level AA**, the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines published by the World Wide Web Consortium. Mobile apps are measured against the same guidelines.

**We do not claim to meet that standard today.** Songcry is partially conformant, which means parts of the product meet it and parts do not. We would rather tell you that than publish a claim we cannot back.

## 3. Where Songcry falls short today

These are the limitations we already know about. We are listing them because you deserve to know before you hit one, not after.

- **Text size.** The app does not yet follow the text size you have set on your device. If you have made system text larger, Songcry will still show its own size.
- **Screen readers.** Parts of the app do not yet work well with a screen reader. The main music feed is the weakest area, because it is built around swiping through full screen video and audio. Many buttons that show only an icon do not yet announce what they do.
- **Captions.** Videos that artists upload do not carry captions or subtitles.
- **Motion and audio.** Songs begin playing on their own as you move through the feed, and video plays with them.
- **This website.** We are reviewing it against the same standard and will report what we find here.

If you hit something that is not on this list, it is not that we decided it was acceptable. It is that we have not found it yet, and we would like you to tell us.

## 4. What we are doing about it

We are working through this in the order that removes the most friction first:

- Reviewing the app with a screen reader and with the accessibility tools Apple provides, and writing down every barrier we find.
- Labelling the buttons that currently announce nothing.
- Making the app follow your device text size.
- Checking colour contrast across the app and this site.
- Designing a proper way through the music feed for people who do not swipe, which is the hardest part and the one we will not rush.

## 5. Tell us about a barrier

If something in Songcry stops you, email **[support@songcry.app](mailto:support@songcry.app)**.

It helps if you can tell us:

- What you were trying to do.
- Where in the app or on the site it happened.
- What device you were using, and whether you had a screen reader, larger text, or another assistive tool turned on.

We aim to reply within five business days. If a fix will take longer than that, we will tell you what we are doing and when to expect it. If we cannot fix something quickly, we will try to help you get it done another way in the meantime.

We treat these reports as a priority rather than as feedback, because a barrier means someone cannot use the product at all.

## 6. Changes to this statement

We will update this page as we fix things and as we find new problems, and the "Last updated" date above will change with it. We will not remove a limitation from section 3 until it is actually fixed.
