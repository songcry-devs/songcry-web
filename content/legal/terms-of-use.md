## Location Features, Permissions, and Safety

The App's core functionality is location-based. To access and use key features of the App, you must enable location services and grant the App permission to access your location (for example, "While Using the App" or another permission level supported by your device). If you do not grant location permission, or if you disable location services, some or all features of the App may not function, and features that rely on location (including content distribution and integrity checks) may be limited or unavailable.

Public display vs backend use. Your precise location is never displayed to other users. Profiles display only the hometown or base city you select. Even where location is not displayed, the App collects and processes location information on the backend to operate the service, including to deliver location-based functionality, detect fraud or artificial engagement, and protect the integrity and security of the App.

With your permission, the App collects precise location from your device. We store your most recent location reading on your account record and use it to assign you to a geographic zone; each new reading replaces the previous one. Our Privacy Policy (songcry.app/legal/privacy) describes in detail what we collect, how it is stored, who can access it, and how long we keep it.

Location data is currently used in: (1) the discovery feed, where songs and content surface based on the user's geographic zone; (2) the distribution radius through which songs travel between listeners; (3) artist analytics, where artists can view approximate city- or region-level listener data; and (4) platform integrity checks. Individual user locations are never shown to other users.

Safety notice: Do not use the App in a way that distracts you or prevents you from obeying traffic, safety, or other laws, including while driving or operating machinery. You are responsible for your own safety and for exercising good judgment when using any location-based features. The App is not an emergency service and you should not rely on it for medical, law enforcement, or emergency assistance. If you need emergency help, call local emergency services.

Do not share precise personal information such as your home address, school, or workplace in comments or other content you post publicly.

For details about what location information is collected, how it is used, and the choices available to you, please review our Privacy Policy (songcry.app/legal/privacy).
