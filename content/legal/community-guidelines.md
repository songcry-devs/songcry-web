Songcry is a fan-first app that helps you discover new music and emerging artists near you. By using Songcry, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines. They apply everywhere in Songcry, including your profile, your songs, comments, and direct messages.

## 1. Be Respectful

Do not harass, threaten, bully, or target others. No hate speech, slurs, or discriminatory content.

## 2. No Impersonation or Deception

Do not impersonate other people, artists, brands, or organizations. Do not mislead users about who you are or what you represent.

## 3. Respect Intellectual Property

Only upload music, artwork, and content you own or have permission to use. Do not upload copyrighted material without rights or authorization.

## 4. Music Made by Real Artists

Songcry is for music made and performed by real artists. We don't accept songs generated with AI tools like Suno or Udio, including AI vocals and AI-generated music. Studio tools that work on your own performance, like mixing, mastering, tuning and effects, are fine. Cloning or imitating another person's voice is never allowed.

## 5. No Unlawful, Dangerous, or Exploitative Content

No content that promotes illegal activity, violence, self-harm, exploitation, or sexual violence. No sexual content involving minors.

## 6. No Doxxing or Sharing Private Information

Do not post or solicit someone's private or sensitive information (addresses, phone numbers, private messages, IDs), or encourage others to do so. This includes posting screenshots of someone's direct messages to shame, harass, or expose them. Do not share your own precise personal information, such as your home, school, or workplace location, in comments or other content you post publicly.

## 7. No Spam or Manipulation

No spam, scams, phishing, fake engagement, or attempts to game rankings, placement, or discovery. No malware or harmful links.

## 8. Location Integrity

Do not falsify or manipulate location signals to misrepresent where content was created or placed. Do not use location features to stalk, intimidate, or target anyone.

## 9. No Harassment

Do not encourage harassment, brigading, or retaliatory behavior. Do not upload content intended to provoke or incite abuse.

## 10. Direct Messages

Direct messages are for real conversations: saying you love someone's song, setting up a collab, planning a show. Everything above applies in messages too, and these rules apply on top:

- A message request is a request. If someone deletes your request, does not accept it, or blocks you, leave it there. Do not message them from another account or ask other people to message them for you.
- No unwanted sexual content. Do not send nudity or sexual content, ask for it, or make sexual comments to someone who has not welcomed them. Never threaten to share someone's intimate images, and never pressure anyone for photos, money, or favors.
- No spam. Do not send the same message to lots of people, paste promotions into chats with people you do not know, or use automated tools to message. Sharing your own music with someone who is interested is fine. Blasting it is not.
- No scams. Do not ask for passwords, verification codes, payment details, money, or gift cards, and do not send links meant to steal accounts or install malware. Songcry will never ask for your password in a message.
- Keep private conversations private. Do not share someone's messages or personal details to harass or embarrass them.
- No contact with minors. Songcry is for adults. If you believe you are talking to someone under 18, stop and report the account with the Minor safety reason.
- No threats, hate, or harassment. Everything in "Be Respectful" and "No Harassment" applies to every message you send.

Messages are checked automatically before they are delivered. Some words and phrases cannot be sent, and photos that appear to show nudity, graphic violence, hate symbols, or drugs are not delivered and go to our team for review.

## 11. Reporting and Blocking

If you see content or behavior that violates these Guidelines, report it in the app or contact us at support@songcry.app. In a conversation, press and hold a message to report it, or use the report option for the whole conversation or account. Reporting sends a copy of the conversation to Songcry for review, and we do not tell the account who reported it. You can also block any account. Blocking stops messages and notifications both ways, and you each stop following the other.

## 12. Enforcement

Songcry has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. We may remove content, remove messages, restrict features including messaging, suspend accounts, or ban users for violations of these Guidelines or the Terms. We report content that appears to involve the sexual exploitation of a child to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children. We may also cooperate with legal requests where required.

## 13. Changes

These Guidelines may be updated over time. The latest version will be posted here with the "Last updated" date.
